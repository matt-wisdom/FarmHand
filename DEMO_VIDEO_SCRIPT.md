## Recording Guidelines
1. **Resolution**: 1080p (1920x1080) at 30 or 60 fps.
2. **Audio**: Use a dedicated headset or USB microphone to ensure clean speech audio.
3. **Screen Setup**: Keep the browser and terminal cleanly side-by-side or use smooth scene transitions.
4. **Duration**: Target exactly between 1:55 and 2:00.
